# Instruction

## Instructions for repo owner
1. Go to the [repo](https://github.com/Contemporary-Physicslab/gitintro)
2. Click the green button `Use this template` and choose `Create a new repository`
3. Choose wisely your repository name - this will also become part of the URL! Don't change any of the other settings and click `Create repository`.

You repository will now be created using the template repo. 

Before your website can be seen, we will need to enable GitHub pages.

3. Go to `Settings` (top mid of the screen) and click `Pages`. From the dropdown menu choose `GitHub Actions` as source. 
4. Click on `Code` in the top left corner and click on ⚙ (the `gear-icon` near **About**) at the right site of the page. Check the box `Use your GitHub Pages` website.


## Inviting your partner
For IP2, you work in pairs. You can invite your partner(s) to collaborate on your repository. To do this, go to your new repository on GitHub and follow the steps below.

Go to your new repository on GitHub.

Click on **Settings** (in the top right corner of your screen).

Click on **Collaborators** in the left-hand menu.

Click the **Add people** button under the "Manage access" heading.

Type your partner's (or partners') username and click on **Add <username> to this repository** (where `<username>` is your partner's username).

Your partner will receive an email with an invitation to collaborate on the repository. Once they accept it, both of you will be able to make changes to the repository.

## Partner accepts invitation
Your partner will receive an email with an invitation to collaborate on the repository. Once accepted, both of you can make changes to the repository.

Follow steps 1 through 6, but use the URL of the repository to which you were invited. Do NOT proceed to step 7, as you want to maintain the link to the existing repository.
